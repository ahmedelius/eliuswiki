Each entry should answer the same questions, so the index stays useful as it grows.

Field What to include
Title The name of the project or experiment.
Year When it was made, or the range of years.
Role What Elius did: design, code, writing, direction, or a combination.
Summary One or two sentences on what it is and why it exists.
Link A public URL, if there is one. Otherwise, the entry stays text-only.
