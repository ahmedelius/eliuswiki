Overview

EliusWiki treats its subject the way an encyclopedia treats a person: one stable page, a clear structure, and facts that can be corrected. The wiki is maintained by Elius and is designed to grow as new work is added.

The project is intentionally small. It does not try to be a social network or a portfolio template. It is a reference page with room for the details that usually get lost between profiles.
