Contact channels

Instagram @ielius_

The public profile for short messages and links.

Email ahmedelius34@gmail.com

Best for corrections, project entries, and longer notes.

What to send

  • Corrections: the page name, the exact text, and what it should say instead.
  • Project entries: a title, year, role, short summary, and a link if one exists.
  • Additions: a fact, a date, or a public link that belongs on a page.
  • Permissions: a request to reuse text or images from the wiki.
  • Collaborations: a short description of the project and what is needed.

Please include a source link when the change is factual. It makes the correction faster to verify and easier to keep.

Response

There is no guaranteed response time. Messages are read when possible, and corrections are applied when they can be verified. A short, specific message is easier to act on than a long one.
